# Provenance and reporting boundary

This repository is an independent laboratory, not part of QEMU.

Its experiments may identify a QEMU, Linux, or hardware-model question, but a
difference is not automatically a bug. Before any external report:

1. reproduce it on the relevant current upstream version;
2. identify the controlling specification or documented contract;
3. validate every memory, privilege, ordering, and synchronization
   precondition;
4. retain the exact source, image hash, command line, trace, and environment;
   and
5. obtain the user's explicit approval before posting anything externally.

QEMU's current policy permits disclosed AI-assisted bug discovery with human
validation, but declines patches believed to include or derive from
AI-generated content. Therefore no source, test, documentation, commit text,
or patch generated with AI assistance here may be repackaged as a QEMU
upstream contribution. See the live policy before any upstream work:
https://www.qemu.org/docs/master/devel/code-provenance.html
